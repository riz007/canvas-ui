# Proof — launch page

Proof is a lid that fits most jars. A small sensor measures how high your sourdough starter
has risen and how warm it is, and tells your phone when it peaks — the moment to bake or feed.
£49, ships in January. Launching to home bakers who already keep a starter and have killed
at least one. The page has one job: get people onto the January pre-order list.
Real numbers: peak is usually 4–12 h after feeding; the lid reads height every 5 minutes, ±1 mm.
