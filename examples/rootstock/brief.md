# Rootstock Coffee — operations dashboard

Two-person specialty roaster in Leeds. 15 kg drum roaster, 4–6 roasts a day, Tue–Thu.
Sells wholesale to ~30 cafés plus a small web shop.
Every morning the head roaster decides: what to roast today, in what order (light to dark),
and whether any green coffee is about to run out. Orders ship the day after roasting.
Today the answers are spread over a spreadsheet, a whiteboard and Shopify.
Used on a laptop next to the roaster (1440×900), glanced at between batches.
