- User: tourist on a phone, 1–3 days before arrival. Main question: will the waves suit a beginner on my day. Main action: book a lesson.
- Round r1 (mobile 390x844): a Tide Table, b First Light, c Pick Your Day.
- Chosen by agent (user away): **a Tide Table**. Not yet confirmed by user.
  - Answers the biggest question first, from content the school already produces (7:30 report).
  - c implies multi-day forecasts the school does not publish; b is the most memorable but buries the report in a pill.
  - Possible borrows: b's big price numerals; c's tide-curve-as-hero.
- Not built: full page, responsive/desktop, motion, states. index.html untouched.
- Mock: report values (0.8–1.0 m, offshore 8 km/h, low 09:42) are illustrative; wire to the real daily post. The "Book" targets are not connected.
