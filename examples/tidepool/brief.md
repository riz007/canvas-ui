# Tidepool Surf School

Small surf school in Ericeira, Portugal. 3 instructors, lessons for complete beginners and improvers.
Lessons: 2h group (€45), 2h private (€90), 5-day course (€190). Boards and wetsuits included.
Most bookings come from tourists on their phones, 1–3 days before arriving.
Biggest question people ask: "will the waves be OK for a beginner on my day?"
We post a daily surf report (wave height, wind, tide) every morning at 7:30.
